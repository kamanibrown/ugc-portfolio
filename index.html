04 / Formats

Pick the job. I’ll bring the format.

Six proven shapes for tech and lifestyle UGC. Bring a script, bring a brief, or bring just the product — hooks and scripting are part of the service.

FORMAT — 01

Problem → Solution Demo

Lead with the pain your buyer already has, then put the fix in their hands. The highest-converting shape for accessories, chargers, storage and desk gear.

15–45s · Hook in first 2s · CTA close

FORMAT — 02

Talking-Head Testimonial

Direct to camera, honest verdict, real use shown on screen. Reads as a genuine recommendation because it’s performed like one — no teleprompter stiffness.

30–60s · On-camera · B-roll cutaways

FORMAT — 03

Unboxing & First Impressions

The out-of-box moment, captured as it happens. Built for launches, where genuine first reactions carry more weight than polish.

30–60s · Macro close-ups · Real reactions

FORMAT — 04

How-To / Tutorial UGC

Teach the feature, don’t pitch it. Step-by-step demos that answer the exact question a buyer types before purchasing — my channel’s signature format.

30–90s · Screen + hands-on · Captioned steps

FORMAT — 05

Comparison & Alternatives

Your product against the default choice, the old way, or the overpriced name brand. Made for buyers in the final compare-and-decide moment.

30–60s · Side-by-side · Verdict close

FORMAT — 06

B-Roll & Voiceover Packs

Clean product footage and voiceover your own editors can cut a dozen ways — macro details, desk-in-use shots, hands-on sequences, no face required.

4K clips · Colour-graded · VO optional

09 / Questions

Before you ask.

Is this a post on your channel?+
No — and that’s the point of UGC. The video is delivered to you to post on your own channels, product pages and ad accounts. If you also want it published to my audience, that’s a sponsored collaboration: see the channel portfolio, or ask about a bundle.
Why don’t you price by followers like other creators?+
Because your ad account doesn’t care how many followers I have — it cares whether the hook holds and the demo converts. UGC here is priced on deliverables, production and the usage window you need. My channel numbers are on this page as proof of craft, not as the price tag.
Can we run the video as a paid ad or whitelist it?+
Yes. Organic posting on your own channels is included in every package. Paid usage — Meta, TikTok Spark, whitelisting and similar — is added as a scoped usage window (30 days up to perpetual) and agreed in writing before filming, so there are no surprises later.
Do we need to write the script?+
Your call. Send a finished script and I’ll perform it naturally, or send a brief with your claims and I’ll write the hooks and script for approval before shooting. Most brands choose the second — hooks are the part I’ve tested for years.
How fast can we get it?+
Your delivery date is agreed at booking, before filming starts, and it’s the date I work to. Need it faster? Rush delivery under 48 hours is available as an add-on when the calendar allows.
What if we need changes?+
Revisions are built into every package (one on a single video, two on the Test Pack) for pacing, caption and CTA adjustments. Raw footage is also available as an add-on if your editors prefer to cut their own versions.
